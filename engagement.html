Engagement Party

February 5–7, 2027 · Austin, Texas

The Plan Where to Stay What to Wear FAQ

The Plan

Friday, February 5: Stand-Up Comedy

When: 8pm
Where: Black Rabbit Comedy ATX
Tickets: Available for purchase here

If you'll be in town early, come see a show with us!

Black Rabbit is one of Austin's underground comedy clubs, located downtown on 5th Street. The venue is BYOB, and there are lots of restaurants and bars within walking distance where you can get dinner and drinks before the show.

Saturday Morning, February 6: Coffee Downtown

When: 9am
Where: Afugã Coffee

If you're eager to see us before the big party, come join our weekend routine: morning coffee and a walk. Afugã is an East Cesar Chavez coffee shop with ample street parking around it, and we'll be hanging out there for a bit.

From Afugã, we plan to take a stroll along Lady Bird Lake on the Ann & Roy Butler Hike & Bike Trail. Sam (and sometimes Patrick) regularly uses this path with her Founders Run Club or when training for triathlons.

Walking west will take us toward the 2nd Street District and South Congress Ave, two great areas for shopping and sightseeing.

Saturday Evening, February 6: The Party

When: 4–8pm
Where: 1204 Salina Street, Apt A, Austin, TX 78702

The party starts at 4pm with snacks, soft drinks, beer, wine, and Tip Top cocktails. We can't wait to have so many of our loved ones in one place, and to watch the Miles and Twohig sides meet each other as we join our families. We'll have a few games to help everyone get to know each other.

Around 5:30pm, dinner arrives from one of our favorite "locals only" barbecue spots, Stiles Switch Barbecue. (Please do not wait in line at Franklin while you're in town.)

Once everybody is good and fed, we'll get a little sappy for a few minutes, let people give some speeches, and have a champagne toast around 7pm.

When the party ends, we'll probably keep the good times rolling with an unofficial after-party at a nearby bar or two.

Sunday Morning, February 7: Hang Out at the Park

When: 9:30am
Where: Mueller Lake Park

Mueller is the neighborhood where we first met, and it's our favorite place to spend a Sunday. If you're local, or don't fly back until the afternoon, we'd love for you to join us. Sundays here are busy, but there are several commercial garages plus plenty of free and metered street parking.

We recommend starting your day with us at our favorite little neighborhood café, Bottega. It's a hidden gem tucked into a more residential part of Mueller, where you're more likely to find free street parking. They roast their own coffee, make incredible breakfast sandwiches from scratch, and always have treats for Bambi.

From there, there are lots of fun things to do within a short walk. We'll set up base camp at Mueller Lake Park with lawn games like bocce and Spikeball, and we encourage you to walk around the lake and check out the Texas Farmers' Market.

Patrick will likely also try to convince you to join him for a pint at BD Riley's, an Irish pub and the go-to spot to watch Boston sports in Austin. Or to see a movie with him at Alamo Drafthouse. It's a real "If You Give a Mouse a Cookie" situation: if you let him go to the park with Sam and Bambi, he's going to keep asking for more of his favorite things.

Where to Stay

We don't have a hotel block. Book whatever suits you, hotel or rental! Here's where we'd look:

East Austin

Closest to the party on Saturday night and to Afugã for Saturday coffee. Lots of rentals and a few boutique hotels, plus great food and bars for the after-party.

Downtown

Walkable to Black Rabbit on Friday and the Lady Bird Lake trail on Saturday morning. The most hotel options in town, and a short ride to everything else.

Mueller

Closest to Pat and Sam's homes. Home base for Sunday at Bottega and Mueller Lake Park. Quieter, more residential, and easy for families.

What to Wear

Bring your cowboy boots and hats! It's Austin, y'all! Austin is a really casual city and lots of cool fashion can be found. Dress comfortably and express yourself!
