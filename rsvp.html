Venue

Pousada Convento Arraiolos

Nossa Sra. da Assunção, 7044-909 Arraiolos, Portugal

It's an old convent turned hotel in the Alentejo, about a mile outside the village of Arraiolos (famous for its handwoven rugs). There's a pool, gardens, and a lot of very old, very pretty walls.

Getting There

Fly into Lisbon (LIS). The venue is about an hour and fifteen minutes from the airport by car.

You don't need to rent a car. We'll run shuttles to and from the venue from the Lisbon airport and from Lisbon itself, so if you want to tack on a few days in the city before or after, go for it. We'll share shuttle times closer to the date.

Where to Stay

Stay with us at the pousada

We're holding rooms at the venue for the whole stay. They run around $200 a night depending on room type. Let us know on your RSVP if you want one so we know how many rooms to hold.
