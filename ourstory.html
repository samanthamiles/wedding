Our Story

It was love at first sight, at least for Patrick… Sam couldn't see anything. Their first date was at L'Oca d'Oro in Mueller on May 4, 2025, on a high-pollen day. Patrick likes to joke that this was all part of his master plan to have Sam fall for him based on his charm and wit before she had a chance to get a good look at him, and he credits himself as the inspiration for Love Is Blind. But the truth is that Sam's allergies that day were just a case of serendipity. Sam was embarrassed by how often she had to excuse herself to go blow her nose, Patrick felt like a dope for not asking for a table inside, and all pretense was dropped. They embraced each other's idiosyncrasies well ahead of schedule.

One of those idiosyncrasies of a shared life would indeed be Sam's overwhelming lead in bathroom visits per restaurant visit. Sam is, in Patrick's estimation, the most hydrated person who has ever lived. He knows this because he plays a central role in maintaining that status: regularly refilling her gigantic water cups, carrying various bottles and cans to the car for every drive, and making her tea every night before bed (with just the right amount of honey). Sam believes Patrick is critically underhydrated, and is determined to change this. Both of them would prefer if Bambi, the service dog they have been raising together, drank more water.

Sam's work with service dogs, along with her work with children, were two of the many things Patrick fell in love with about her. Her strong will, her open-heartedness, and her worldliness impress him every day, and he brags about them whenever he gets the chance to tell someone about her. What Sam loves most about Patrick is his tenderness and loyalty. Behind the Boston grit is someone who cares deeply, fiercely, and completely, and who would do anything for the people he loves.

After their dinner at L'Oca d'Oro, Patrick and Sam went for a walk around Mueller Lake Park. Evening (and morning) walks like this have become part of their routine together, whether in their own neighborhoods, in parks around town, or in the cities they've explored together. On the rarest occasion, they even run. After a few months of dating, Patrick picked Sam up early one evening from a friend's wedding to head downtown for a comedy show, and they parked at Patrick's office. In the car that night, she said she loved him for the first time, but she said it rapidly in Spanish (a language he does not speak) and then ran off to the comedy show. Patrick ran after her, because he loved her too, and would chase after her anywhere.

Going to comedy shows is another of Sam and Pat's favorite things to do together, because they love hearing each other laugh. When they're not leaving it to the professionals, they have a series of inside jokes to entertain themselves. Some are not fit for polite company, and most are so absurd they aren't worth explaining. Patrick's insistence that they keep their eyes closed for at least one shot any time they take a photo is one of the stranger bits Sam has embraced, with the goal of compiling a gag photobook they can pass on to their grandkids.

Toward the end of their first date, Patrick and Sam talked about their shared belief in dating with intention. Sam had said that 2025 was going to be the year she met her future husband. She was exactly right, even if she wouldn't see him clearly until their second date. After almost a year and a half of dating, Patrick proposed on Friday, October 16, at Chapultepec Castle in Mexico City. He still couldn't speak Spanish, but he knew implicitly how much they loved each other.

Past performance is not indicative of future results. But we're feeling good about this one.
